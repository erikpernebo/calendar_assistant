# Persistent goals

Standing commitments the scheduler protects across the whole semester.

Tiers:
- **1** — immovable. Coursework routes around it.
- **2** — defended. Cut only when a deadline is inside 48h, and the cut gets logged.
- **3** — opportunistic. Fills leftover time in listed order.
